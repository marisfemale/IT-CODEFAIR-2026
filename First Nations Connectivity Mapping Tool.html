First Nations Connectivity Mapping Tool


The First Nations Connectivity Mapping Tool is an interactive map that provides geospatial information about the accessibility of various facilities, resources, connectivity, and demographic statistics for First Nations communities across Australia.


The map is designed for visualising, exploring, and analysing data. It allows the user to zoom into specific areas of interest, view pop-ups, and access as well as export layer information.


The map is regularly reviewed and updated by the Department of Infrastructure, Transport, Regional Development, Communications, Sport and the Arts in cooperation with various Australian agencies.
